Orbital Breach

A free zero-G multiplayer shooter in your browser, with team modes from 1v1 to 20v20 and solo matches against bots.

Choose Start to load the game. If Start stays unavailable, reload this page and check that JavaScript and WebGL are available.

Freeze the squad. Breach the portal.

Team Cyan and Team Magenta launch from opposite breach rooms into a zero-gravity debris arena. Grab rails to redirect your drift and fire a freeze pistol at enemy pilots.

Win a round by entering the enemy breach room or freezing every enemy pilot. The first team to 5 points wins the match. A 120-second round timeout is a tie with no point awarded.

Desktop controls

  • Mouse to look; WASD to walk inside breach rooms.
  • E to grab or release a rail; Space to jump in breach rooms.
  • Hold Space while grabbing to charge a launch; move the mouse while charging to aim launch power, then release Space to launch.
  • Left mouse button to fire; V to switch first-person / third-person view; hold B for look-back view.
  • Hold Tab for the scoreboard; Esc for the session menu / release cursor; H for help.

Touch controls

Use the movement joystick in breach rooms and drag the look area to look around. Tap GRAB near a rail. Use JUMP to jump, or hold LAUNCH while grabbing and drag down to charge power; release to launch. Hold FIRE to shoot. The 1ST/3RD button switches camera view.

Before you play

Use a browser with JavaScript and WebGL, with a keyboard and mouse or a touchscreen. No install or account is required. Touch controls support portrait and landscape; landscape gives the controls more room. Online rooms require a network connection; solo bot matches and a tutorial are also available from the menu.
